01 · The problem

Anyone can build an agent.
Almost no one can sell one.

Workflow builders automate your own work. The moment a useful agent should serve someone else, you are rebuilding hosting, accounts, API keys, metering, billing, and trust by hand.

And the next buyer is often another agent. It can't fill out a checkout form or wait for an API key. It needs a price, a payment rail, and a receipt it can read.

02 · Why now

The rails for agents to pay each other just arrived.

New buyers

Agents are buying

Agent-driven commerce is projected at $190B–$385B in US GMV by 2030.

New rails

Payments standardized

x402 per-call USDC, the Agentic Commerce Protocol, A2A and MCP give agents a common way to discover, call and pay.

New trust

On-chain identity

ERC-8004 identity, reputation and validation let a buyer check who it is paying before it pays.

The question isn't who can build an agent. It's where agents get built, priced and paid.

03 · What Agent Studio is

One canvas. Every flow is a product.

Wire specialized agents on a visual canvas, then launch the flow as a paid service in one step. Humans pay by card; agents pay per call in USDC. Every launch is discoverable over MCP, A2A, OpenAPI and x402.

08 · Business model

Creators keep 100%. We earn on compute.

Live

Model usage margin

Flows run models through the Suede gateway at cost plus a margin. Revenue grows with every run, paid or free.

Live

Card credits

Humans top up by card. Committed-use credit packs trade a discount for prepaid volume.

Lever

Take rate on paid calls

Currently 0% so creators keep every dollar while supply builds. It can be switched on once the marketplace has density.

Funnel

Free tier

100,000 free model tokens per workspace each month, then pay as you go.
