01 The problem

Creation got cheap. Ownership didn't scale.

Cost to createAI era

Heading toward zero.

AI drove the cost of making songs, images, video and synthetic media toward zero. Value is leaving production, and the layer that proves authorship, carries rights and routes payment is breaking.

What ownership needsUpstream

Machine-readable, portable.

Copyright protects ownership after distribution. AI-era media moves faster than that. Ownership has to be machine-readable, upstream and portable across humans and agents.

02 Why now

Three curves converging in 2026.

01Supply shock

AI-media volume

Generated media is exploding across every format. Provenance can't keep up by hand.

02New buyers

Agent commerce

Autonomous agents now pay per call for content and APIs. They need machine-readable rights.

03New rails

On-chain standards

ERC-8004 identity, reputation & validation mature into a usable ownership substrate.

The question isn't whether another tool can generate media. It's who owns the rails when that media has to be proven, licensed and paid out.

The agent-native rights layer.

Machine-readable proof of creation and on-chain royalty routing, so autonomous AI agents can discover, license and pay for creative work. Live on Base mainnet: the rail agents transact through for IP.
